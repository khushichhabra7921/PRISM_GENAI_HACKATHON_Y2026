## 6. Known Edge Cases & System Limitations
* **No-match log (bench/cluster_nomatch.py).** 14 distinct no-match queries were logged during the run: the 10 `queries.json` complaints with no SIIS coverage (Galaxy Watch sync, WhatsApp transfer, repair price, account sign-in, S Pen, eSIM, DeX, Wallet, bootloader, water damage), each its own theme, plus 4 complaints whose topic IS covered but that the conservative gate rejected ('phone is tapping things on its own', and three held-out paraphrases seen on the cold path). With this few distinct gaps the clustering yields singleton themes; it is meant for production-volume logs.
* **Synthetic data.** Catalog, SIIS articles, queries and gold samples were generated for this prototype; SIIS menu paths follow one consistent `Settings > A > B` style, which flatters the deterministic step parser and the screen-resolution numbers. Official SIIS text with free-form phrasing will need `app/steps.py` patterns extended.
* **Step parser coverage.** Steps are copied from source sentences (no hallucination), so an instruction phrased as a statement rather than an imperative is skipped, and very long sentences stay as one step.
* **Cache false hits vs recall.** Lower τ raises paraphrase hit rate but lets semantically-near different problems (e.g. 'phone slow' vs 'phone slow after update') share a plan; see the τ sweep. Negatives are tracked separately.
* **Multi-symptom detection** relies on conjunction splitting (rules) or the SLM's symptom list; complaints that describe two issues without a conjunction are answered with one goal.
* **Categories.** `manual` also covers non-physical actions that have no Settings screen (e.g. updating apps from a store), because the contract has only three categories and manual actions may not carry deeplinks.
* **CPU-only latency** varies with power state (battery vs mains) and thermal throttling; the environment line above records the state during this run.
* **validationDeeplink** is left null: the catalog provides validation keys, but the desired target value of a toggle cannot be derived safely from the SIIS text alone.
